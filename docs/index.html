"The questions are always more important than the answers." — Randy Pausch, The Last Lecture
I am a member of the leadership team in Microsoft's AI Development Acceleration Program (MAIDAP) at the Microsoft New England Research & Development (NERD AI) center. I lead the teams to drive the state-of-the-art in AI by developing advanced AI capabilities, products, and solutions applicable across the enterprise, as well as growing the next generation of AI leaders who can make a transformational impact to the world.

Previously, I was an Executive Director of AI at CVS Health, where I led the team responsible for AI-based clinical care planning initiatives. Prior to that, I was a Senior Scientist and Technical Lead of the AI Group at Philips Research North America, where I focused on solving various Natural Language Processing (NLP) problems such as Clinical Information Extraction, Clinical Question Answering, Natural Language Inference, Paraphrase Generation, and Medical Image Caption Generation using Deep Learning.

Before joining Philips, I was an NSERC Post-Doctoral Fellow at the University of Lethbridge, Canada, where I received my PhD and MSc in Computer Science with specialization in NLP and Machine Learning. My PhD dissertation, entitled "Complex Question Answering: Minimizing the Gaps and Beyond", was nominated for the 2014 CAGS/UMI Distinguished Doctoral Dissertation Award.
